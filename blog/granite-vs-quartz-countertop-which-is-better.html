Granite vs Quartz Countertop — The Honest Verdict

By Shree Shyam Stonex 2 December 2024 7 min read

Search results for “granite vs quartz” are overwhelmingly written by either granite suppliers or quartz brands. This one is written by a granite manufacturer who will tell you exactly when quartz is the right answer.

The honest head-to-head

CriterionGraniteQuartz
Material origin100% natural igneous~93% quartz + 7% resin/pigment
Heat resistanceExcellent (250°C+)Poor — resin softens above ~150°C
Scratch resistanceExcellentExcellent
Stain resistanceGood (sealed)Excellent (non-porous)
MaintenanceSeal every 12–24 monthsZero sealing
UV / outdoor useYesNo — resin yellows
RepairabilityRe-polish on site possibleDifficult — usually replace
PatternEvery slab uniqueRepeatable, uniform

Cost (installed, India 2025)₹180–1,000 per sq.ft₹500–1,400 per sq.ft
Resale impactHigher in India (perceived premium)Neutral

When granite wins (most cases)

  • You cook with high heat (Indian kitchens regularly hit 200°C+ pans)
  • The countertop is exposed to direct sunlight
  • You want a stone that lasts as long as the building
  • You value visual uniqueness over uniformity
  • Budget per sq.ft matters

When quartz wins

  • You want a perfectly uniform, light-coloured surface (whites, light greys)
  • Zero maintenance is a hard requirement (rental property, busy household)
  • Sealing is something you will genuinely never do
  • Bathroom vanity where stain resistance trumps everything

The myths

  • “Quartz is more hygienic than granite.” Both, when sealed, are food-safe. The hygiene difference is marginal.
  • “Granite is high maintenance.” Sealing takes 5 minutes once every 12–24 months.
  • “Quartz lasts forever.” The resin component degrades. Quartz countertops over 25 years old are rare.
  • “Granite is dated.” Marketing line from quartz brands. Dark granite in leathered finish is one of 2025’s most-specified materials.

The verdict

For a working Indian kitchen built to last 30+ years: granite, ideally Coin Black Lapatro or Jeerawal White. For a low-use rental kitchen, white-themed bathroom vanity, or a household where nobody will ever seal anything: quartz.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is more expensive — granite or quartz?

On average, quartz is more expensive than standard granite at installed cost. Premium exotic granites overlap with quartz pricing.

Does granite need to be sealed every year?

Dense granite (Jet Black, Coin Black) needs sealing every 18–24 months. Medium-density varieties like Jeerawal White need it every 12–18 months.

Can I use quartz outdoors?

No. UV exposure causes the resin in quartz to yellow and degrade. For outdoor surfaces, use granite.
