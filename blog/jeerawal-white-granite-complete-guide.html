Product Spotlight

Jeerawal White Granite — The Complete Guide

By Shree Shyam Stonex 22 January 2025 6 min read

Jeerawal White is one of the most consistently specified white granites coming out of Rajasthan. It carries the brightness of premium imported stones at a fraction of the cost, with a track record now stretching back two decades.

What it actually looks like

A bright off-white base with finely distributed grey-black mineral speckles and occasional warm beige tones. Under polished finish, the base reads almost ivory; under honed, it shifts cooler and more contemporary.

Technical specifications

PropertyTypical value
Density2,680–2,720 kg/m³
Water absorption (ASTM C97)0.15–0.22%
Compressive strength (C170)205–235 MPa
Flexural strength (C880)17–22 MPa
Mohs hardness6–7
Available thickness16mm, 18mm, 20mm, 30mm
Standard slab size240–280cm × 60–90cm (cutter)
Gangsaw slab sizeup to 320×180cm

Where it works best

  • Kitchen countertops — pairs well with both wood and lacquer cabinetry
  • Bathroom vanities and shower walls
  • Living room flooring — especially with under-floor heating
  • Hotel lobbies needing brightness without the cost of marble

Common finishes

Polished is the most common (85% of orders). Honed is gaining share in modern minimal projects. Leathered is available on request but loses some of the stone’s characteristic brightness.

Comparable alternatives

  • P. White — slightly darker speckling, more economical
  • Imperial White — more pronounced grey veining
  • Kashmir White — warmer beige tone, South Indian quarry

2025 price range (ex-factory Kishangarh)

  • 18mm polished: ₹110–180 per sq.ft
  • 20mm polished: ₹130–210 per sq.ft
  • 30mm polished (kitchen-grade): ₹180–320 per sq.ft
  • Premium block-matched lots: +30–50%

What to ask before ordering

  1. Bundle-wise photos of the specific lot
  2. Number of slabs from a single block (book-matching potential)
  3. Edge consistency — no fissures on cutter edges
  4. Sealer recommendation (Jeerawal is mildly porous and benefits from initial sealing)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Jeerawal White granite good for kitchens?

Yes, with proper sealing. It is dense enough for daily kitchen use and pairs beautifully with most cabinetry styles. Initial sealing and reseal every 12–18 months is recommended.

What is the price of Jeerawal White granite in 2025?

Ex-factory Kishangarh: ₹110–180 per sq.ft for 18mm polished, ₹130–210 for 20mm. Premium book-matched lots can be 30–50% higher.
