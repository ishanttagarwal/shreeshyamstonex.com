Best Granite Colors for Flooring in 2025

By Shree Shyam Stonex 18 December 2024 6 min read

Flooring is the largest continuous surface in any interior, which makes colour choice high-stakes. These are the granite colours specified most often by interior designers across India in 2025 — and the rooms each one suits.

1. Jeerawal White — the modern default

Bright off-white base with fine grey speckles. Expands rooms visually, pairs with every cabinet style, takes warm and cool lighting equally well. Best for living rooms, kitchens, and master bedrooms with natural light.

2. P. White — the value choice

Slightly more pronounced grey-black speckling than Jeerawal. Excellent durability for high-traffic areas at a more accessible price. Ideal for hallways, staircases, and rental properties.

3. Jet Black — bold and modern

Reads as monolithic and contemporary. Use in entrance lobbies, powder rooms, and home theatres. Avoid in small rooms without strong lighting.

4. Tiger Brown — warm and grounded

Brown base with dramatic dark veining. Works beautifully with warm wood tones and Mediterranean interiors. Best for living rooms and bedrooms in homes with natural materials elsewhere.

5. Bagera Brown — luxury statement floor

Rich brown with bold patterning. Most often used in formal dining rooms, hotel lobbies, and feature corridors where you want the floor to be the centerpiece.

6. Coin Black Lapatro — tactile contemporary

Leathered finish hides foot traffic marks better than polished. Increasingly specified for kitchens, bathrooms and walk-in wardrobes in premium residential.

7. Cateyes — exotic accent

Unique natural patterning. Use sparingly — entrance vestibule, single feature corridor, lift lobby. Not recommended for large continuous floors as patterns can feel busy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which colour granite is best for floor?

Jeerawal White and P. White are the most versatile flooring choices for Indian homes. Tiger Brown works well in warm-toned interiors, while Jet Black suits contemporary low-light spaces like entrance lobbies.

Is dark granite floor a bad idea for small rooms?

Dark granite reads heavy in small or poorly lit rooms. Reserve dark stones for spaces with strong natural or artificial light, or use them as accents rather than across the entire floor.
