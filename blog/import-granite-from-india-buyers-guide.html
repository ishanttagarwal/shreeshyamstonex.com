Importing Granite from India: A Complete Guide for Overseas Buyers

By Shree Shyam Stonex 5 May 2025 11 min read

India is the world’s second-largest granite exporter. Most overseas buyers, however, are quoted prices without ever seeing the documentation, freight, or quality-control mechanics behind the transaction. This guide demystifies the entire process.

1. HS codes you need to know

  • 2516.12.00 — Granite, merely cut into blocks or slabs of a rectangular shape
  • 6802.23.00 — Worked granite (polished, edged, cut to size)
  • 6802.93.00 — Other worked granite (countertops, special shapes)

The correct HS code determines duty calculation in your country. Most worked granite imports fall under 6802.23.00.

2. Standard export documentation

  1. Commercial Invoice
  2. Packing List (with slab dimensions, gross/net weight per bundle)
  3. Bill of Lading (sea) or Airway Bill
  4. Certificate of Origin (issued by Indian Chamber of Commerce or APEDA)
  5. Phytosanitary Certificate (where wooden crating is used)
  6. Quality Test Report (ASTM C97 absorption, C170 strength, C99 hardness)
  7. Pre-Shipment Inspection Report (recommended for first orders)

3. Container loading capacity

Container20mm slabs (approx)Net weight
20ft Standard180–220 slabs (~450 sq.m)~24 tonnes
40ft Standard380–440 slabs (~900 sq.m)~26 tonnes (volume capped)
40ft High Cube400–460 slabs~26 tonnes

Granite is dense (around 2,700–2,800 kg/m³), so container loading is almost always weight-limited, not volume-limited. Mixing thicknesses optimises load.

4. Common ports of export from India

  • Mundra (Gujarat) — closest to Kishangarh, most cost-effective for Middle East and Europe
  • Nhava Sheva (Mumbai) — widest carrier coverage
  • Chennai — preferred for South-Asia, Far-East and US East Coast
  • Krishnapatnam — emerging for South Indian stones

5. Indicative transit times from Mundra

  • Jebel Ali (UAE): 8–12 days
  • Dammam (KSA): 10–14 days
  • Birgunj (Nepal, via road from Kolkata): 18–22 days
  • Hamburg (Germany): 28–34 days
  • New York (USA): 30–38 days

6. Quality checks before shipping

Insist on these five before payment of the balance:

  1. Bundle-wise slab photos with measuring tape visible
  2. Lot consistency check (block-matched within a bundle)
  3. Edge integrity inspection (no chips, cracks, fissures)
  4. Polish gloss meter reading (typically 85–95 GU for polished)
  5. Wooden crating photos before container stuffing

7. Payment terms standard in the industry

Most Indian granite exporters work on:

  • 30% advance against pro-forma invoice
  • 70% balance against scanned Bill of Lading copy

Letter of Credit (irrevocable, at sight) is used for first-time orders above USD 50,000 and is the safest instrument for both parties.

8. Red flags when sourcing

  • No GST registration or IEC (Import-Export Code) shared
  • Refusal to share bundle photos before shipping
  • Quotes significantly below market without explaining processing or thickness
  • No physical factory address — only a trading desk

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the HS code for granite countertops?

Worked granite (cut, edged, polished countertops) generally falls under HS code 6802.23.00. Unworked blocks fall under 2516.12.00.

How many granite slabs fit in a 20ft container?

Approximately 180–220 slabs of 20mm thickness, weight-limited at around 24 tonnes.

What is the cheapest port to import Indian granite to the Middle East?

Mundra (Gujarat) is typically the most cost-effective Indian port for Middle East destinations because of proximity to Kishangarh and direct carrier services to Jebel Ali and Dammam.
