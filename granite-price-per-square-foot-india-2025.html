Granite Price per Square Foot in India — 2025 Rate Guide

By Shree Shyam Stonex 20 February 2025 7 min read

Granite pricing in India varies more than buyers expect — the same slab name can sell for ₹150 or ₹450 per square foot depending on thickness, finish, lot, and supplier. Here is what 2025 rates actually look like, ex-factory Kishangarh.

2025 indicative rates (ex-Kishangarh, polished, 18mm)

VarietyPrice range (per sq.ft, INR)Best for
Jet Black₹90 – ₹180Countertops, vanities, flooring
P. White₹70 – ₹140Flooring, staircases
Jeerawal White₹110 – ₹220Kitchen, feature walls
Coin Black Lapatro₹180 – ₹340Premium kitchen islands
Tiger Brown₹140 – ₹280Floors, facades
Bagera Brown₹180 – ₹360Luxury interiors
Cateyes (exotic)₹280 – ₹600+Feature walls, statement surfaces

Ex-factory, before transport, installation, edging and taxes. Premium book-matched lots and 30mm thickness will trend toward the upper end of each range.

What actually moves the price

  1. Thickness: 18mm → 20mm adds 12–18%. 20mm → 30mm adds 35–55%.
  2. Finish: Leathered (Lapatro) and honed finishes add 10–25% over polished.
  3. Lot grade: Top-grade block-matched lots can be 30–60% above standard.
  4. Edge work: Mitred or laminated edges add ₹200–500 per running foot.
  5. Quantity: Below 500 sq.ft buyers should expect 8–15% premium on the rate above.

Installed cost in India

Add the following to ex-factory rates for installed cost:

  • Transport (within India): ₹15–45 per sq.ft
  • Cutting and edging: ₹40–120 per sq.ft
  • Installation labour: ₹60–120 per sq.ft
  • GST: 18%

How to negotiate without insulting the supplier

  • Ask for “lot rate” instead of “sq.ft rate” — you’ll see better numbers.
  • Bundle thickness and finish in one order to consolidate machine time.
  • Pay 50% advance instead of 30% for an additional 3–5% discount.
  • Lift directly from the factory yard if your transport is local.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cheapest granite in India?

P. White and standard Jet Black are typically the most affordable, starting around ₹70–90 per square foot ex-factory Kishangarh in 2025.

Why does the same granite have different prices?

Thickness, finish, lot grade, edge work, and order quantity all change the per-square-foot rate. Block-matched premium lots can cost up to 60% more than standard.
